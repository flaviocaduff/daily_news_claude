Swiss housing costs rose sharply in 2024; disposable income stagnated

  • Swiss housing costs rose to CHF 1,546 per month in 2024 from CHF 1,449 in 2023, according to the Federal Statistical Office household budget survey.
  • Housing and energy costs took 14.8% of gross income and remained the largest household spending item.
  • Total consumption averaged CHF 5,244 per month, or 50.2% of gross income.
  • Mandatory costs were about one third of income, led by taxes at CHF 1,274 per month (12.2%).
  • Average disposable income barely moved, from CHF 7,186 to 7,209 per month; average monthly savings were CHF 1,578 (15.1%).
  • Lowest-income households often spent more than they earned, and 61% of households sit below the average disposable income.

Critical Apache mod_http2 Flaw CVE-2026-119618 Patched in 2.4.71

  • Apache released HTTP Server 2.4.71 on 11 Nov 2026 to fix CVE-2026-119618, a heap corruption flaw in mod_http2 that Apache says can lead to RCE.
  • Affects versions 2.4.55 through 2.4.70. It is triggerable over HTTP/2 without authentication, so only servers with HTTP/2 enabled are exposed.
  • Severity is disputed: Red Hat and Amazon score it 9.8 (CVSS v3.1), CISA 8.1. Red Hat rates its RHEL 9 and 10 builds Important due to a separate allocator.
  • Anthropic's OSS Scanner (reported 26 Oct) and Google Cloud Vulnerability Research (3 Nov) found the bug independently.
  • Public PoC shows heap corruption, not RCE. No confirmed in-the-wild exploitation and not in CISA KEV. Some distros, including SUSE and openSUSE, still await fixes.

TLDR InfoSec

NoisyTCP: 11-year-old Linux kernel flaw enables remote server crashes

  • Hyunwoo Kim disclosed NoisyTCP, an 11-year-old Linux kernel TCP flaw (CVE-2026-99288) affecting almost all distributions.
  • Unauthenticated remote attackers send crafted TCP packets to trigger a NULL pointer dereference and kernel panic. No data alteration or code execution.
  • Default Nginx, Apache or SSH servers receiving TCP directly are exposed. The race window is narrow; multi-queue or RPS setups are easier to hit.
  • A patch was merged into mainline two weeks ago. Interim workarounds: disable SYN cookies, which weakens SYN flood defence, and use a single RX queue.
  • Kim previously disclosed Dirty Frag (May, local root escalation) and Januscape (July, VM escape in a 16-year-old kernel flaw).
